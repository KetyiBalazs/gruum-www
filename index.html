More sources of truth, more silent drift.

Design tokens live in Figma. Components live in code. Copy lives in a doc somewhere. The more people — and agents — touch the product, the further those drift apart, and nobody notices until the product looks stitched together. Gruum catches it before your users do.

◇ Who it’s for

Built for everyone who touches the system.

◇ Design

The design system owner

Problem You maintain the source of truth, but you can’t see it drift once it ships. Every team copies a component, tweaks a color “just this once,” and six months later nobody’s sure what’s actually canonical.

Solution Gruum scans the live product against your system and shows exactly where it diverged — so you catch drift without auditing every PR by hand.

◇ Engineering

The product / frontend engineer

Problem You inherit a codebase full of near-duplicate colors and quietly-forked components from months of fast shipping. Cleaning it up never makes it to the top of the backlog.

Solution Gruum finds the duplicates and hands you a prompt that maps each one back to the right token — or merges them into a new one — so cleanup takes minutes, not a sprint.

◇ Agents

The vibe coder / solo builder shipping with AI agents

Problem Every feature your agent builds might invent a new hex code, spacing value, or component instead of reusing what’s already there. The drift is invisible until the product looks like five different apps stitched together.

Solution Gruum checks each scan against your real system and gives your agent a prompt to fix what it got wrong — so it builds on what exists instead of reinventing it.

◇ Product

One scan, four layers of consistency.

◇ Styles

Colors, type & effects

Finds hardcoded hex values, near-duplicate colors, inconsistent text styles, and styles nobody’s using anymore — and tells you which should merge, and which should just go.

◇ Components

Component drift

Flags buttons, cards, and inputs that are almost the same but not quite — too many variants, or a component quietly forked in two places.

◇ Copy & tone

Voice consistency

Surfaces copy that doesn’t match your established tone — inconsistent capitalization, terminology, or button-label patterns.

◇ Animation

Motion consistency

Catches transitions and micro-interactions that don’t match your system’s timing, easing, or motion language.

◇ Proof

From scattered hex codes to one source of truth.

A real component, before and after a Gruum scan. Every hardcoded color gets traced back to an existing token — or, where none exists yet, merged into a new one.

◇ How it works

From repo to report in minutes.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Log in and connect your GitHub repo, or point Gruum at your live site.

  2. 02

    Scan

    Gruum checks your styles, components, copy, and animation for drift, duplication, and anything that’s gone unused.

  3. 03

    Prompt

    Get a report plus a ready-to-run prompt: what to merge, what to delete, and where each hardcoded value should map back to. Hand it to your developer, or feed it straight to your coding agent.
